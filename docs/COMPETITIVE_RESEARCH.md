## Market context

- In the DVSA October 2025 instructor survey, 3,333 respondents reported an **average of 18.1 learners actively being trained**. This is an average, not a maximum or a distribution of all solo instructors; nevertheless, a proposed DriveTrack Solo cap of 10 risks excluding an ordinary workload. [DVSA survey](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/working-as-a-driving-instructor-survey-2025-results/working-as-a-driving-instructor-survey-2025-results)
- A third first-party price anchor, Total Drive, currently lists instructor plans at £18, £23, £24 and £26/month (including VAT), with a 30-day full-access trial. Online booking appears on the £23+ plans. [Total Drive membership](https://totaldrive.co.uk/instructor-software-pricing/)

## Implications to test, not claims of superiority

- **“Pick up where you left off” alone is not unique.** Both competitors advertise lesson records and continuity. A possible DriveTrack wedge is an exceptionally fast **instructor-facing next-lesson preparation loop**: private observation → chosen next focus → pre-lesson recap → post-lesson update. That needs a working product comparison and instructor testing before it can support premium pricing. [DrivoPilot progress](https://drivopilot.com/pupil-progress), [Passdesk home](https://passdesk.co.uk/)
- **Controlled slot release is not automatically unique.** Passdesk explicitly says learners book only slots an instructor opens. DriveTrack would need demonstrably clearer controls, fewer taps or a specific workflow advantage. [Passdesk calendar](https://passdesk.co.uk/features/calendar/), [student portal](https://passdesk.co.uk/features/student-portal/)
- **A no-card 14-day trial may reduce sign-up friction versus Passdesk, but not versus DrivoPilot.** It is a useful acquisition choice, not a defensible product differentiator. [Passdesk pricing](https://passdesk.co.uk/pricing/), [DrivoPilot pricing](https://drivopilot.com/pricing)
- **A £30–£50 Solo price with only 10 active learners is not supported by these benchmarks.** It would need evidence of unusually strong time savings or outcomes. Validate active-learner counts, willingness to pay and actual workflow preference with instructors before setting caps or public prices. [DVSA survey](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/working-as-a-driving-instructor-survey-2025-results/working-as-a-driving-instructor-survey-2025-results), [DrivoPilot pricing](https://drivopilot.com/pricing), [Passdesk pricing](https://passdesk.co.uk/pricing/), [Total Drive membership](https://totaldrive.co.uk/instructor-software-pricing/)

Unknown from public pages: real product reliability, speed/usability of each workflow, active customer numbers, support quality, conversion or retention, and instructors' willingness to pay for DriveTrack. Do not market a competitor feature as absent on this evidence.
